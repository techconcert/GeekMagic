What is new in GeekQuest r8:

- Deep missions: once a day (between 09:30 and 18:30) the hero can go on a long expedition of 20 to 30 connected screens through a generated forest, cave or ruins dungeon. Each level is different, nothing is stored for it.
- Three monster types in every biome (forest: slime, bat, crab; cave: bat, grey slime, grey crab; ruins: ghost, red slime, dark bat, plus the boss at the end).
- The hero fights, opens chests and comes home with goods and a heart; a mission is capped at 60 minutes or 90 room changes so it always ends.
- The bottom band shows progress, for example "DEEP CAVES 12/24".
- A mission in progress survives a power cut (it resumes at the last saved room).
- The browser preview has a "Deep mission" button with a biome picker to try it.
